## 1. Title Strategy: The 3 Distinct Approaches

Never force all video titles into the same formulaic pattern. For every long-form and Shorts release, the system generates three distinct title candidates:

1. **Search-Oriented (Discovery Focus):**
   * *Formula:* `[Key Historical Figure / Civilizational Site]: [Precise Milestone or Full Documentary]`
   * *Example:* `राजेंद्र चोल की नौसेना: 1025 CE का संपूर्ण ऐतिहासिक सच`
   * *Purpose:* Captures organic YouTube and Google search intent with high clarity.
2. **Story-Oriented (Narrative Focus):**
   * *Formula:* `[Conflict, Betrayal, or Rise/Fall] of [Kingdom / Figure]`
   * *Example:* `हल्दीघाटी का महासंग्राम: महाराणा प्रताप और अकबर की निर्णायक टक्कर`
   * *Purpose:* Hooks viewers interested in dramatic military and political stakes.
3. **Curiosity-Oriented (Investigation Focus):**
   * *Formula:* `[Paradox, Archaeological Anomaly, or Historical Contradiction]`
   * *Example:* `दिल्ली का लौह स्तंभ: 1600 साल बाद भी जंग क्यों नहीं लगा?`
   * *Purpose:* Prompts immediate click-through on Browse and Suggested video feeds.

### Absolute Title Constraints:
* **No Deceptive Clickbait:** The title must deliver on the promise within the first 60 seconds of narration.
* **Length Bounds:** Optimal length is 45–70 characters (avoids truncation on mobile screens; hard cap 100).
* **No Forced Prefixes:** Never automatically prepend `"The Secret of"` to titles.

## 2. Thumbnail Composition Standards

Thumbnails are rendered at **1280×720** (long-form) and **1080×1920** (Shorts portrait).

### Core Visual Principles:
* **One Dominant Focal Point:** A single high-contrast subject (e.g. ancient warrior statue, monolithic temple facade, golden relic) occupying at least 45% of the frame.
* **High Contrast & Clarity:** Clear figure-ground separation with soft edge vignetting and dark underlays.
* **Limited On-Screen Text:** Maximum 2 to 4 words. Use `*stars*` to highlight the single most compelling word (e.g. `*Kailash* Temple`, `*Naval* Empire`).
* **Devanagari Typography:** For Hindi thumbnails, the system automatically selects `Rozha One` or `Noto Sans Devanagari Bold` with clean drop-shadow outlines.
* **No Cheap Clutter:** Generic red arrows, exaggerated emoji faces, and words like `"SHOCKING"` are strictly banned.
